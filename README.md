## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
FarmConnect is an AI-powered digital marketplace designed to connect farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers.

The platform provides a user-friendly application where farmers can register, upload their available agricultural products, set prices, and receive orders directly from customers.

Consumers can browse available products, compare prices, and purchase fresh agricultural products. Hotels, supermarkets, restaurants, and wholesale buyers can place bulk orders through the platform.

The system also integrates artificial intelligence to predict product demand and recommend suitable delivery routes.

Key features:
1. Farmer and buyer registration.
2. Direct buying and selling of agricultural products.
3. Real-time product availability and price updates.
4. Bulk ordering facility.
5. AI-based demand forecasting.
6. Smart logistics and route optimization.
7. Order tracking and notifications.

### Innovation and Uniqueness:

Unlike traditional agricultural marketplaces, FarmConnect combines direct farmer-to-consumer trading with AI-powered demand prediction and logistics optimization in a single platform.

### 2. Methodology and process for implementation <b>(Flow Charts/Images/ working prototype)</b>

Methodology and Implementation Process:
1. User Registration: Farmers, consumers, and bulk buyers create accounts.
2. Product Listing: Farmers upload product details, quantity, images, and prices.
3. Product Browsing: Buyers search and select available agricultural products.
4. Order Management: Buyers place orders, and farmers receive order notifications.
5. Demand Forecasting: AI analyzes historical sales and predicts future demand.
6. Route Optimization: The system identifies efficient delivery routes.
7. Order Completion: Products are delivered to customers, and order status is updated.

## Feasibility and Viability
### 1. Analysis of the feasibility of the 

FarmConnect is technically and economically feasible because it can be developed using commonly available open-source technologies and existing digital infrastructure.

The platform can initially be implemented in a small agricultural area and gradually expanded to multiple villages and cities.

### 2. Potential challenges and risks

- Limited digital knowledge among some farmers.
- Internet connectivity problems in rural areas.
- Maintaining accurate product availability.
- Transportation and delivery coordination.
- Building trust between farmers and buyers.

### 3. Strategies for overcoming these challenges

- Provide a simple and multilingual user interface.
- Offer mobile-friendly access.
- Update product availability regularly.
- Connect with local delivery partners.
- Introduce transparent pricing and order tracking.

## Impact and Benefits
### 1. Potential impact on the target audience

The FarmConnect platform can create a positive impact on farmers, consumers, bulk buyers, and the agricultural industry by improving the way agricultural products are purchased, sold, and delivered.

1. Impact on Farmers
- Helps farmers sell their products directly to consumers without depending on multiple middlemen.
- Increases farmers' income by providing better selling prices.
- Provides access to a wider customer base.
- Helps farmers understand market demand through AI-based demand forecasting.
- Reduces the risk of unsold agricultural products.
2. Impact on Consumers
- Provides fresh fruits and vegetables at affordable prices.
- Allows consumers to purchase directly from farmers.
- Offers more product choices and transparent pricing.
- Improves access to quality agricultural products.
3. Impact on Bulk Buyers
- Helps hotels, supermarkets, restaurants, and wholesalers purchase products directly from farmers.
- Makes bulk ordering easier and faster.
- Reduces purchasing costs.
- Provides information about product availability and quantities.
4. Impact on the Agricultural Industry
- Encourages digital transformation in agriculture.
- Improves supply chain management.
- Reduces food wastage through demand prediction.

- Promotes sustainable agricultural practices.
